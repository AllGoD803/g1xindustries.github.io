# G1X — technical and economic pre-feasibility

Version: 2026-09-24. Public working study, not an engineering certification or company solvency assessment.

## Scope

- Audit of the existing homepage and four French technology posters, including every displayed HydroCore, AeroChannel, SolarUltra, GEN1 Nexus, Balises, GeoScan and TechOps concept.
- Software-first homepage with existing Filaments and Budget demos intact.
- Seven separate bilingual technical briefs, bilingual study, printable complete report, three assumption calculators.
- Official G1X logo and approved concept images preserved. Illustrative measurements and unsupported product specifications removed from the main visitor path.

## Evidence boundaries

Manufacturer specifications are component-level evidence. No new live access to K12, Pi nodes, ORICO or sensors occurred in this study. No field test, site measurement, supplier installation quote or paying-customer validation has been performed.

The reported hardware-cost ranges and labour hours are planning allowances, not verified market quotations. Public calculations use fictional economic scenarios and no private financial data. Hardware purchase, certification and winter qualification remain separate gates.

Primary references, consulted 2026-09-24, are in `sources.json` and linked from the public pages. Source pages establish physical principles and component limits; G1X assumptions and arithmetic are explicitly identified.

## Navigation

The older standalone demos continue to work. Legacy home hash links redirect to their new equivalent. Existing poster assets remain archived but are not marketed as verified capabilities.

## Validation

Static checks cover unique IDs, single H1, internal links, bilingual text coverage, wrapped content and accidental exposure of known private identifiers. Calculator cases cover battery scaling, hydro units, zero flow, positive margin, increased acquisition cost and nonpositive margin. Browser validation covers published navigation, rendering, language switch, scenario editing and mobile overflow.

No tracking, third-party script, account, payment handling or machine connection was introduced.
